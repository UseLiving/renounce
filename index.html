01

Launch for free

Pick a ticker, a name and a countdown of 5 to 30 minutes, then share it. The site pays the launch fee and there is no dev wallet. Until the countdown ends anyone can join the launch basket; one transaction then launches the coin and buys it for the whole basket at one price.

02

Trade on the curve

Buy and sell with SOL on a bonding curve. Fees are a 1% curve fee and a 0.25% app fee, half of which goes to the trader's referrer. A full curve graduates the coin to a locked liquidity pool.

03

Burns, with receipts

Of every trade's creator fee, 50% buys back the coin and 30% buys back $RNCD, and both are burned. At a random moment 20 to 30 minutes after its last burn, each coin burns its whole bucket once it holds at least 0.01 SOL.
